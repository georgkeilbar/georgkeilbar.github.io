Research

I develop statistical methods for modern data science, with a focus on giving machine-learning tools a rigorous statistical footing: nonparametric theory and valid inference for explainable ML (such as Shapley curves), and inferential guarantees for stochastic gradient descent and high-dimensional data. On the applied side, I work on distributional and causal methods and panel data, with applications to economic inequality in Germany, financial systemic risk, LLM-generated text and biomedical data.
